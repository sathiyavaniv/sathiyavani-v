## 🔍 Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sample** | 188 employees across IT service companies (software development, IT support/ops, data/analytics, management) |
| **Design** | Descriptive, cross-sectional survey design |
| **Instrument** | Structured questionnaire — demographics + 20 five-point Likert-scale items across 4 constructs |
| **Analysis Tool** | IBM SPSS Statistics |
| **Core Question** | Does technology adoption improve operational efficiency, and what role do organizational readiness and employee training play? |

## 🧩 Research Framework

```
                    ┌────────────────────────┐
                    │   Technology Adoption   │──┐
                    └────────────────────────┘  │
                    ┌────────────────────────┐  │      ┌──────────────────────┐
                    │ Organizational Readiness │──┼─────▶│ Operational Efficiency│
                    └────────────────────────┘  │      └──────────────────────┘
                    ┌────────────────────────┐  │
                    │Employee Training & Skills│──┘
                    └────────────────────────┘
```

**Hypotheses tested:**
1. **H1** — Technology adoption positively influences operational efficiency
2. **H2** — Organizational readiness moderates the technology adoption → efficiency link
3. **H3** — Employee training and skills improve efficiency through better technology utilization

## 🏆 Key Findings

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| 🔒 **Reliability (Cronbach's α)** | 0.951 – 0.958 per construct · **0.975 overall** (excellent internal consistency) |
| 🔗 **Strongest correlation** | Employee Training & Skills ↔ Operational Efficiency (**r = 0.764**, p < 0.01) |
| 📈 **Combined predictive power** | TA + OR + ET explain **68.4%** of variance in Operational Efficiency (R² = 0.684, F = 132.478, p < 0.001) |
| 🥇 **Top individual predictor** | Employee Training & Skills (β = 0.386, p < 0.001) |
| 🥈 **Second predictor** | Technology Adoption (β = 0.341, p < 0.001) |
| 👍 **Productivity perception** | 84.6% agree technology adoption improved their productivity |
| 💰 **Cost impact** | 80.3% agree technology adoption reduced operational costs |

## 🧪 Methodology

| Objective | Statistical Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Gender vs. Operational Efficiency Level | Chi-Square Test of Independence | χ² = 10.67, df = 2, p = 0.005 |
| Relationship among TA, OR, ET, OE | Pearson Correlation | r = 0.660 – 0.769, p < 0.01 |
| Employee Training → Operational Efficiency | Simple Linear Regression | R² = 0.583, F(1,186) = 260.49, p < 0.001 |
| Operational Efficiency across Age Groups | One-Way ANOVA (Tukey HSD) | F(4,183) = 7.054, p = 0.001 |
| TA + OR + ET → Operational Efficiency *(primary objective)* | Multiple Linear Regression | R² = 0.684, F(3,184) = 132.48, p < 0.001 |
